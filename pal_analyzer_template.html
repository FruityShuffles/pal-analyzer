Breeding paths

What could you make that beats what you own? Buyable passives are already handled by the planner — breeding is the only way to get the unbuyable ones (Legend, Lunker, Lucky, the element emperors), so the search is about landing those on a species worth having them.
Projected IVs are the expected result of inheritance, not a best case: two 100-IV parents average 79, so a bred Pal usually loses IVs against one you have already maxed. Eggs is the average number you need to hatch before the wanted passives land; it counts hatches, not hours. A bred Pal starts at 0★ / 0 souls / T0.
